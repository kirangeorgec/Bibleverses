Privacy Policy for The Holy Bible Verses

Effective date: 23 October 2026

The Holy Bible Verses ("the app") is developed by Kiran George. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, share or sell any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Information stored on your device

When you save a verse with a feeling, or add your own feeling, that information is stored only on your device, inside the app. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Permissions and sharing

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. If you use the Share or Copy features, the verse text is passed to the app you choose, under that app's own privacy policy.

Internet use

The Bible text is built into the app and works offline. The app does not send your information over the internet.

Children's privacy

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
